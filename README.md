# Human-Reviewed  
This label marks projects that, regardless of how they were created, were finally reviewed, understood, and verified by humans.

## What it means:
This label identifies projects whose code may have been created, in part or in full,
with the assistance of AI (or some thing else, which is not a human beeing), but has been reviewed by at least one
human. 

"Human-Reviewed" here includes that the whole project (all files in repo) is / are:

- Reviewed in terms of dependencies and licenses 
- Verified in its logic and comprehensibility
- Executed or tested and and understood in its function
- Checked for obvious security issues 

**by at least one human beeing**

## In short: 
So there is at least one human beeing who understands the project and how it works completly and who can trace bugs, errors, or unexpected behavior without any additional tools.  
This person has seen and understood every file and every line of code and decided that all files should remain exactly as they are or kept modifying them until that decision could be made.

## How to use: 
If the criteria apply to your project and you want to show this, please add the badge or copy it from here and include a short link to this repo. 

## Some tohught about the label and why it exists:
The label is a self-declaration and does not guarantee the absence of errors. 
Absolutely not — people make mistakes and learn from them, so it's to be expected that there will be errors or bugs in the project and code, and that's perfectly fine.

Programming, just like being human, is a never-ending story of trial and error (and, in the best-case scenario, improvement).  
Not AI or whatever else might come along to create perfection (if it even exists).  
And unless humans has been absorbed, wiped out, or lost their humanity, humans will continue to debug not only today but also tomorrow.
